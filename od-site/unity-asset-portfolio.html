About / Working approach

Solo built. With an eye for the workflow.

As a solo developer I build Unity tools that lower the barrier to entry and stay out of the way in daily use. I'm interested in the small frictions: a click too many, a body that disappears in first person, or documentation that doesn't help on first open.

Less friction, more building — with documentation verified against the shipped source. Easy FP Full Body Controller is vedaloiv's first released asset; the Procedural Dungeon Generator is next.
