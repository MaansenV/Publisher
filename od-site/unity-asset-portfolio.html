Easy FP Full Body Controller

True first person with a visible body. Look down and you see your legs; reach for a door and your hands do.

  • Stable anchor camera with procedural hand, head and spine IK
  • Ground-anchored foot planting and finger grips per item
  • Item system with holster; FPCutter hides the head in first person but keeps its shadow
  • 7-step Setup Wizard, 3 demo scenes and ready-made prefabs
  • Optional health and heart-rate stamina; FishNet and PurrNet add-ons for 2–4 player co-op

  • Node-based templates and seeded random generation
  • Multiple floors and infinite chunk streaming
  • Rule-based decoration, portal culling and runtime NavMesh
  • 4 demo scenes

One developer, built around the workflow.

I build Unity tools that lower the barrier to entry and stay out of the way in daily use. The interesting problems are the small frictions: a click too many, a body that disappears in first person, documentation that doesn't help the first time you open it.

So every tool gets a guided setup, demo scenes to learn from, and docs checked against the shipped source.

  • Guided setupWizards and sensible defaults get you to a working scene quickly.
  • Docs checked against sourceEvery documented option exists in the shipped package.
  • One asset, one manualPer-asset docs with quick starts, demos and changelogs.
  • Real products onlyNo invented numbers, no promised features.
