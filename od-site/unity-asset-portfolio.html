Easy FP Full Body Controller

True first person with a visible body. Look down and you see your legs; reach for a door and your hands do.

  • A steady camera while your hands, head and spine follow what you do (procedural IK)
  • Feet plant on the ground and fingers grip each item (foot planting, per-item grips)
  • Items with a holster; your head is hidden from your own camera but still casts a shadow (FPCutter)
  • Optional health and heart-rate stamina, plus 2–4 player co-op through FishNet or PurrNet add-ons

From import to Play

Watch the walkthrough

  1. Import the package

    Into a URP project on Unity 6.5 or newer. Input System and Cinemachine 3 are required.

  2. Run the Setup Wizard

    Tools › FBSystem › Setup Wizard builds your player in 7 steps.

  3. Press Play

    Open one of the 3 demo scenes and walk around with a full body in first person.

v1.1.0Released 8 Oct 2026

Easy Dungeon Generator

Paint the rooms, connect them in a graph, press generate. Works for hand-tuned dungeons and endless backrooms alike.

  • Paint floors, doors, stairs and holes on a grid (Room Designer)
  • Connect rooms in a node graph; the same seed gives the same layout (seeded generation)
  • Several floors, or endless levels loaded in pieces (infinite chunk streaming)
  • Decoration placed by rules, plus portal culling and a runtime NavMesh

From rooms to a dungeon

  1. Paint your rooms

    Create a theme, then paint rooms in Tools › DungeonGen › Room Designer.

  2. Connect them in a template

    Lay out the room graph in Tools › DungeonGen › Dungeon Template Designer.

  3. Generate

    In Tools › DungeonGen › Dungeon Setup, create a template dungeon, assign the template and rooms, and click Generate Dungeon. 4 demo scenes show finished setups.

New in 1.1.0: wall mode and double-sided walls, room doors, the Room Prefab Manager and 4 demo scenes. Full changelog

One developer, built around the workflow.

I build Unity tools that lower the barrier to entry and stay out of the way in daily use. The interesting problems are the small frictions: a click too many, a body that disappears in first person, documentation that doesn't help the first time you open it.

So every tool gets a guided setup, demo scenes to learn from, and docs checked against the shipped source.

  • Guided setupWizards and sensible defaults get you to a working scene quickly.
  • Docs checked against sourceEvery documented option exists in the shipped package.
  • One asset, one manualPer-asset docs with quick starts, demos and changelogs.
  • Real products onlyNo invented numbers, no promised features.
