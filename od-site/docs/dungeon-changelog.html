1.1.0 Latest#

Released 2026-10-08.

Walls and doors#

  • Double-sided walls and ceilings. The DungeonTheme has the new options doubleSidedWalls and doubleSidedCeilings. Saved rooms record whether their walls are double-sided. See Wall mode.
  • Wall Mode on DungeonManager: SingleOnly, DoubleOnly, and AllowBoth. Connected rooms can share one wall at a seam, with Allowed Seam Overlap to line them up.
  • Room Door brush in the Room Designer (P, with I to invert). It places a fixed two-sided door with no connector.
  • Entrance seam padding: useEntranceSeamPadding and entranceSeamPadding override the seam padding for rooms attached to the scene entrance.

Room authoring#

  • Room Prefab Manager (Tools › DungeonGen › Room Prefab Manager) links Room Design assets to their prefabs and regenerates them, for example after a theme change.
  • Bake Decor in the Room Designer stores the furniture inside the saved prefab, instead of placing it at runtime.

Generation and streaming#

  • Generation status: DungeonManager.Status, IsGenerating, GenerationProgress, and the StatusChanged event.
  • Frame budget: generationFrameBudgetMs spreads room placement and decoration over frames in Play Mode. prewarmOnAwake prepares the prefabs in Awake.
  • Chunk streaming: ChunkDungeonStreamer.WorldChanged event.

Demo scenes and documentation#

  • Four demo scenes: 01_TemplateDungeon, 02_InfiniteDungeon, 03_CullingAndNavMesh, and 04_DecorationAndRoomDesigner. They need the free art pack. See Demo Scenes.
  • The demo guide Documentation/Demos.md is included.
Upgrading from 1.0

Rooms saved with 1.0 do not record double-sided walls. To use DoubleOnly or AllowBoth, turn on Double-Sided Walls in the theme and save the room prefabs again.

1.0 Initial release#

The package export is dated 2026-08-29.

  • DungeonManager with TemplateExact and TemplateSequencedRandom generation.
  • Room Designer with floors, doors, stairs, and holes, and the Dungeon Template Designer.
  • Dungeon Theme Wizard, Dungeon Setup, and Setup Room Prefab.
  • Decoration profiles with runtime decoration.
  • Chunk streaming with ChunkDungeonStreamer.
  • Portal culling (DungeonCulling) and the NavMesh post-processor.
  • Optional Burst planner, progression items, and connection gates.
