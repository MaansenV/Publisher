Overview

Easy FP Full Body Controller

Documentation for v1.6.0 URP Unity 6000.5+

A first- and third-person full-body character controller for Unity 6.5 and URP. The body is animated and driven by procedural IK. You see your own arms, items sit in your hands, and your feet stay planted on stairs and slopes.

Features

Setup WizardSeven steps from your Humanoid model to a working player, with Transfer Configuration to reuse a tuned setup. Body IK and foot plantingHead, spine and hand IK, feet anchored to the ground with re-steps, finger grips and footsteps. ItemsEquip, holster, use and drop items, with per-item grips, item definitions and IK presets. FPCutterHide the head and other body parts in first person while they still cast shadows. Vitals and staminaOptional health, damage and revive, plus heart-rate based sprint stamina. DemosThree guided scenes and two ready-made prefabs for a fast start. Events and APIC# events, UnityEvents and listener interfaces, with the public components. Multiplayer add-onsOptional FishNet 4.7.2 and PurrNet v1.22.0 add-ons with their own Setup Wizards.

Quick start

Drop the demo player and camera rig into a scene with a floor and press Play. For your own character, run the Setup Wizard on a Humanoid model. The Quick Start has both paths step by step.
