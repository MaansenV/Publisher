What changed in each release of Easy FP Full Body Controller, newest first. Breaking changes are marked. Each release's upgrade notes list what to do before you update a project, and the notes for older releases are at the end.

The full changelog ships in the package as CHANGELOG.md.

1.7.0 Latest

Released 2026-10-28.

Added

  • Item IK Presets window. Lists every item of a player and assigns, loads and saves an IK preset per item. See Manage presets for all items.
  • Hand sway and walking motion. The held hand trails view turns, breathes, bobs with each step and leans into strafes. See Hand sway and walking motion.
  • Foot planting turns and stops. Planted feet keep their heading while the body turns. A foot left turned after you stop settles with a re-step. See Turning and stopping.
  • Stride warping. The step length follows the body's real speed, so a standing foot does not slide. It needs the baked stride table. See Stride warping.
  • Eased movement and body turns. Starting, stopping and turning accelerate instead of jumping to the target speed. A fast look turns the view ahead, and the body follows.
  • Scripting hooks: PlayerCameraController.SetYawLimit and SetViewPoseOffset, PlayerInputHandler.SetMovementSuppressed, and PlayerSpineStabilizer.PostureBend.

Changed

  • Crouch locomotion is reworked. The shipped crouchSpeed is 1 (it was 2), and crouchBackDiagonalSpeed sets the diagonal backward speed.
  • Idle, walk and run use the repaired Locomotion_Standing_*.anim clips, so planted feet stay put when you stop and turn fast.
  • A hand at its reach limit moves without a kink. The clamp eases in over reachSoftBand.

Fixed

  • Feet at different heights. With one foot on a ledge, the higher leg no longer folds into a deep squat: the pelvis drops less so every leg keeps minLegExtension (0.55 of its length) where the lower foot can still reach the ground.
  • Crossed feet after a turn at a ledge. Both feet could wait for each other to step and stay crossed. A planted foot now counts as standing when it holds its own anchor, and the foot that stepped longer ago goes first.
  • Standing up under low ceilings. The stand-up check sweeps the top of the capsule, so a shallow door header keeps the player crouched.
  • Main demo scene. The second character lost its model, and the player lacked foot planting and finger grips. The scene now uses the ready-made prefabs.
  • Network add-ons. Remote players receive the owner's look yaw, and the PurrNet and FishNet pickup and drop checks are hardened.

Upgrade notes

  1. Reset the step curves. Existing components keep their stored path, arc, lift and rotation curves. New components get the new defaults. To apply them to an existing component, open Advanced Settings, expand Re-Steps and press Reset Step Curves.

  2. Bake the stride table. Until it is baked, there is no stride warping. Select the player and run Tools › FBSystem › Foot Planting › Bake Stride Speeds. Bake again after you change the locomotion clips, and on characters that use the shipped controller.

  3. Movement eases in and out. To get the previous instant response, set moveAcceleration and moveDeceleration to 0 on PlayerSettings.

  4. The body lags a fast look. To make the body follow the view at once, as before, set bodyTurnSmoothTime to 0.

  5. Check Crouch Speed. Existing PlayerSettings assets keep their value. Check Crouch Speed after you update.

  6. Re-import the add-on. Re-import the FishNet or PurrNet add-on after you update the base package.

1.6.0

Released 2026-10-09.

Added

  • Demo scenes and ready-made prefabs. FBSystem_Player and FBSystem_CameraRig, plus three guided scenes. See Demos.
  • Smooth equip and unequip (holster). Switching items lowers the current item out of view, then raises the new one. Two-handed grips fade the left hand on and off. See Items.
  • Finger grips with HandPoseController, reusable FingerPose templates and per-item tweaks. The grips calibrate from the avatar's muscle limits. See Finger grips.
  • Footsteps with PlayerFootsteps, detected from the animated feet. See Footsteps.
  • Item definitions and categories (ItemDefinition, ItemCategory). See Definitions and categories.
  • Health and stamina (optional) with PlayerVitals and PlayerExertion. See Vitals and Stamina.
  • PlayerInputHandler.InteractTriggered and PlayerCameraController.IsLocalCameraActive.
  • The PurrNet and FishNet add-ons were rebuilt for this version, including holster and drop sync and item definitions.

Changed

  • Breaking Foot IK is now foot planting. The component is PlayerFootPlanting, and the settings have new names. The foot settings of your scenes and prefabs reset to their defaults. Details are in the upgrade notes.
  • Event timing with smooth transitions. ItemUnequipped fires while the item is still visible. ItemEquipped can fire for an item that is still waiting to be drawn. OnEquip and OnUnequip only fire for items that are shown. Turn off Smooth Transitions to get the instant behaviour back.
  • UseCurrentItem() is ignored until the new item has been drawn.
  • Foot planting runs earlier (execution order −200), so hands no longer drift relative to the camera on stairs and uneven ground.
  • Holding sprint while standing still no longer counts as sprinting.
  • Setup tools find the package folder from their own location, so moving FBSystem no longer breaks generated assets.

Fixed

  • SetInputEnabled(false) left the player walking with the last input, and kept Jump and Flashlight presses latched.
  • The FPCutter invisible shader works on GLES, WebGL and Android. Hidden parts could render magenta there before. It is SRP Batcher compatible and casts correct spot and point light shadows.
  • FPCutter fallback material uses the invisible shader instead of a visible Lit shader.
  • The Setup Wizard's Create action for Player Settings failed when the settings folder did not exist. The validator's auto-fix could overwrite an existing settings asset.
  • Inspectors no longer throw errors after a domain reload with a stale selection.
  • The flashlight world item showed Item as its display name.

1.4.0 2026-09-08

Added

  • Ground-anchored foot IK with automatic re-steps and a direct three-bone leg solver. It was first named PlayerFootGlue; it is PlayerFootPlanting since 1.6.
  • PurrNet multiplayer add-on for host-client co-op with 2 to 4 players, on PurrNet v1.22.0.
  • FPCutter multi-mesh support. The wizard lists every SkinnedMeshRenderer of the character and processes the selected ones with a shared skeleton. The generated copy is a complete character.

Changed

  • Segmented FPCutter meshes recalculate their bounds, so skinned-mesh culling stays correct.

Removed

  • Breaking The legacy foot IK systems, including the csHomebrewIK integration, were removed. Scenes with the old components must be migrated. See the upgrade notes.

1.3.0 2026-08-28

Added

  • Character Configuration Transfer. Copies the tuning and missing items of a finished player to a new character. See Transfer Configuration.

1.1.0 2026-07-30

Added

  • Extensible event API. Each equip, unequip and locomotion change fires a C# event, a UnityEvent and any listener interface on the player, once per transition. See Event API.
  • The demo PlayerEventLogger logs every event to the Console.

Changed

  • HandItemSocket is the single source of equip and unequip events. ItemContainer drives the socket and no longer emits its own equip events.

Removed

  • Breaking HandItemSocket.OnItemAttached and OnItemDetached were renamed. The container's own OnItemEquipped and OnItemUnequipped events were removed.

1.0.0 Initial release

First-person and third-person full-body controller with procedural IK, the Setup Wizard, FPCutter mesh cutting for first-person body-part hiding, and a demo scene with item pickup and drop.

Upgrade notes

Read the notes for every version between your current version and the new one. Re-import the add-on packages you use after you update the base package.

Upgrading from 1.3 (includes 1.4 and 1.6)

Breaking changes

Read these steps before you update a project that uses 1.3. Keep a note of your foot settings first.

  1. Remove the old foot IK components (1.4). Delete the legacy foot IK components from the player, including any csHomebrewIK integration. Then run Tools › FBSystem › Setup Wizard, which adds the foot component.

  2. Foot IK is now foot planting (1.6). Renamed types and members: PlayerFootGlue to PlayerFootPlanting, PlayerLegSolver (new name for the solver), GlueMode to PlantMode, StepParams to StepSettings, PlayerController.FootGlue to FootPlanting, ResetGlue() to ResetPlanting().

    Renamed inspector settings in 1.6

    The settings have clearer names. Examples: Enable Foot Planting, Anchor Capture Range, Max Leg Extension, Lift Height, Arc Strength and Catch-Up Boost. Existing components stay attached, because script GUIDs did not change.

  3. Foot settings reset to their defaults (1.6). Note your tuned values, then enter them again after the update. Use the defaults if you did not tune them. If an old PlayerFootGlue entry shows as a missing script, remove it and add PlayerFootPlanting. Foot settings do not carry over.

  4. Item events changed (1.6). Items switch with a holster animation. Check any code or Inspector wiring that expects the old timing. See the 1.6.0 list above. To keep the old instant switch, turn off Smooth Transitions on HandItemSocket.

  5. Sprint while standing still no longer counts (1.6). Animator and SprintStarted only fire while the player moves.

  6. Re-import the add-on (1.6). Re-import the FishNet or PurrNet add-on after the update. Older add-on packages do not compile against 1.6. The add-ons do not synchronize health.

Upgrading from 1.0 to 1.1

  • Inspector wiring: re-wire OnItemAttached to OnItemEquipped and OnItemDetached to OnItemUnequipped. The payload is the same, the equipped item's GameObject.
  • Code: rename the subscriptions. For the container's removed events, subscribe to HandItemSocket.ItemEquipped or ItemUnequipped. The slot index is in ItemEquipArgs.SlotIndex.
  • ItemHoldData.OnEquip, OnUnequip and OnUse are unchanged.
All renamed members in 1.6
  • PlayerFootGlue → PlayerFootPlanting
  • PlayerFootGlueSolver → PlayerLegSolver
  • PlayerFootGlueEditor → PlayerFootPlantingEditor
  • GlueMode → PlantMode
  • StepParams → StepSettings
  • PlayerController.FootGlue → PlayerController.FootPlanting
  • ResetGlue() → ResetPlanting()
